## Brand & Style

The design system embodies **The Neon Editorial** aesthetic—a synthesis of high-end editorial discipline and kinetic neo-fintech energy. It rejects standard corporate banking tropes in favor of an authoritative yet hyper-fluent financial operating environment designed for modern families and digital-native wealth builders.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Vivid Simplex:** Visual clarity through structural simplicity. Radical reliance on negative space, deep dark-mode tonal values, and razor-sharp typographic scale to frame dynamic capital flows without structural clutter.
- **Kinetic Fluency:** High-chroma, light-emitting neon accents (Electric Lime, Vivid Neon Purple, Cyber Cyan, and Outflow Coral) set against an ink-black canvas, communicating status, safety, growth, and burn at a glance.
- **Negative Space as Structure:** The strict **No-Line Rule** eliminates 1px dividing rules and generic horizontal separators. Groupings and structural hierarchies are communicated exclusively through surface shifts, stacked squircle elevations, and low-opacity ghost boundaries.
- **Tactile Obsidian Glass:** Floating utility layers, navigation hubs, and overlays utilize frosted obsidian glass with intense backdrop blurs to establish continuous optical depth across multi-layered data.

## Colors

The color system is calibrated around an OLED Black canvas (`#0A0A0C`) optimized for dynamic contrast, reduced optical fatigue, and luminous accents.

### Hierarchy & Semantics
- **Primary Accent (`#CCFF00` - Neon Electric Lime):** Denotes primary user agency, active targets, positive velocity, budget health indicators, and high-priority action triggers.
- **Secondary Accent (`#BF5AF2` - Vivid Neon Purple):** Reserved exclusively for wealth generation, investment portfolios, automated SIP triggers, and intelligence-driven rule banners.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#00F5FF` - Cyber Cyan):** Signifies liquid balance security, safe-to-spend envelopes, protection umbrella policies, and verified allocations.
- **Danger & Outflow Accent (`#FF453A` - Vivid Coral):** Applied strictly to capital outflows, high burn warnings, impending commitment expirations, and negative ledger values.
- **Neutral Hierarchy:**
  - `canvas-base` (`#0A0A0C`): Baseline application canvas and full-bleed backgrounds.
  - `surface-01` (`#141418`): First-level cards, data modules, and primary squircle containers.
  - `surface-02` (`#1C1C22`): Nested input cards, modular tray elements, and secondary list rows.
  - `surface-03` (`#24242C`): Active hover states, segmented pills, and chip containers.
  - `text-primary` (`#FFFFFF`): Numbers, headlines, and primary data labels.
  - `text-muted` (`#8E8E93`): Category taxonomy, micro-timestamps, and secondary metrics.

## Typography

The typographical pairing provides immediate structural tension between tech-editorial punch and financial clarity:

1. **Expressive Numerals & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Applied to currency amounts, display metrics, key data summaries, and section headers. Features intentional negative letter spacing (`-0.03em`) on large numbers to maintain high visual mass and brutalist digital confidence.
2. **System Precision & Data Labels (`Manrope`):** Delivers neutral, readable execution for secondary values, transactional metadata, member badges, and ledger logs. 
3. **The Editorial Lockup Rule:** Display amounts (`display-lg-mobile`) must always be anchored by a high-contrast uppercase `label-sm` metadata tracker positioned either directly above or grouped into adjacent multi-column summary strips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 4-column system for mobile viewports, scaling to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 12-column layout on desktop dashboards. 

### Spacing Principles
- **Negative Space as Segmentation:** Spacing jumps replace rules and dividing borders. Use `space-md` (16px) for item separation inside cards, and `space-xl` (32px) to detach modular cards and summary charts across the vertical scroll.
- **Mobile Density:** On mobile viewports, the horizontal margin is locked to 16px (`1rem`), with an 80px fixed bottom offset to clear the floating frosted glass navigation bar.
- **Card Padding Hierarchy:** High-level dashboard cards maintain internal padding of `space-lg` (24px) for prominent breathing room, while nested ledger rows and interactive list units collapse to `space-md` (16px) internally.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy operates strictly via **Tonal Mass and Frosted Luminance**, avoiding traditional blurred black drop-shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Level 0 (`#0A0A0C`):** Non-elevated system foundation. Content rests directly on the deep ink-black background.
- **Tier 1 Container (`#141418`):** Squircles raised via luminance shift. Enclosed by a crisp ghost outline: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 2 Container (`#1C1C22`):** Nested contextual cards, active form fields, and modal containers. Uses subtle internal elevation over Tier 1.
- **Glassmorphic Floating Chrome:**
  - Bottom navigation bar, floating action overlays, and quick-filter switches use `rgba(10, 10, 12, 0.8)` with a `20px` backdrop filter blur and an upper perimeter border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Chroma Glow (Kinetic Elevation):**
  - High-value elements, primary focus states, and goal completions cast a diffused aura rather than a dark shadow. 
  - Accent aura formula: `0px 8px 32px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.15)` for Electric Lime active buttons, and `0px 8px 32px rgba(191, 90, 242, 0.12)` for Wealth containers.

## Shapes

The design system implements a rounded squircle shape language to soften high-density financial metrics:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** 20px squircle border radius (`rounded-xl` / 1.25rem - 1.5rem scaled).
- **Interactive Buttons & Pill CTAs:** 14px rounded-pill / squircle radius (`rounded-lg` / 0.875rem) to ensure a distinct tactile identity separate from background containers.
- **System Chips & Segmented Selectors:** Full continuous pill radius (`9999px`) for contextual member badges, filter tokens, and category tags.
- **Form Controls:** 12px squircle radius for input envelopes, maintaining consistency with nested container contours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Solid `#CCFF00` (Electric Lime) fill, `#0A0A0C` bold text, 14px radius, 0px border. Includes an ambient Lime glow (`box-shadow: 0px 4px 20px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.25)`) on hover and active states.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Transparent background with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text in `#FFFFFF`. On hover, background shifts to `#1C1C22`.
- **Pill Switchers:** 9999px radius, filled with `#1C1C22`, active state highlighted with `#CCFF00` or `#BF5AF2` text and subtle `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` fill.

### Cards & Grouping
- **Strict No-Line Ledger:** Cards do not use internal divider rules. Grouping is expressed through 12px–16px vertical gap spacing and tonal switches from `surface-01` (`#141418`) to nested `surface-02` (`#1C1C22`).
- **Ghost Outlines:** Container boundaries rely on a single outer hairline: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Status Accent Rings:** Hero cards display radial ambient accent lighting matching their financial role (Cyan glow for liquid cash, Purple glow for investments).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Number Pad & Currency Keypads:** Display currency in `display-lg-mobile` (`Space Grotesk`) with a blinking `#CCFF00` cursor. 
- **Text & Dropdown Inputs:** Containers filled with `#1C1C22`, no default borders. Active input state is indicated by a focused 2px bottom indicator line in `#BF5AF2` or `#CCFF00` rather than a full outer box outline.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 6px squircle checkboxes and fully circular radios filled with `#1C1C22`. Active state fills with `#CCFF00` accompanied by dark glyph icons.

### Chips & Member Avatars
- **Member Category Chips:** Full pill radius (`9999px`), 10px `Manrope` uppercase font, tracking `0.08em`.
- **Attribution Indicators:** Stacked mini circular avatars with high-contrast borders (`#0A0A0C` 2px ring) to visually link shared family transactions.

### Data Visualization & Velocity Gauges
- **Zero-Axis Policy:** Charts and telemetry graphs exclude structural grid lines and Y/X axis strokes. Values rely on inline data badges, terminal labels, and variable line weights (3px–4px curved neon lines).
- **Progress Bars:** 8px thick tracks using `surface-02` (`#1C1C22`), filled with linear gradient fills (`#CCFF00` to `#00F5FF` for budgets, `#BF5AF2` to `#CCFF00` for savings velocity).
